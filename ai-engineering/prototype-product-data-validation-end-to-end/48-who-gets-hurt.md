## Task 1: claims to check before anything is built on this table

Read against the rows above, the captures in `assets/` and the background hunts r1 and r2 (2026-09-30).

**Seen directly, safe to build on**

- The snack's title and pack flavour disagree, and its brand line names another company (seen by hand).
- The protein powder's title shortens the brand and the flavour; its brand line and pack spell them in full (seen by hand).
- The serum's brand is spelt "Bionike" on the page and "BioNike" on the pack (capture `48-brand-format.png`).
- Flomist's second gallery slot is the Medicines Act placeholder, and its name leaves out 12 ml and 120 doses (capture `48-placeholder-image.png`).

**Jumps from what was seen to a claim, marked**

- Every "Who is harmed" and "How the decision is affected" cell is a possibility reasoned from the page, not an observed customer outcome.
- "May not see the product" in the snack row assumes the brand filter reads the brand line; the filter was not tested.
- No row establishes a §11 hard block. The snack row cites §11 because its image and title disagree; which of the two is wrong is unknown, and the client decides whether it blocks publication.
- The Flomist and lipstick rows depend on client rules not yet known: whether a placeholder counts as an image, and whether the parent record or each shade is judged.

**Not found, kept visible**

- Variant pages disagreeing: none among 29 compared by the background hunt, not confirmed by eye.
