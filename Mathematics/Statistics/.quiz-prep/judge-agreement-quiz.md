## Words this uses

Navigation: ⬅️ [Position](#position) | 📋 [Table of contents](#table-of-contents) | [What the grades rest on](#what-the-grades-rest-on) ➡️

**Covered** (node verdict): at least one kind holds a passage graded full. The format calls such a node a leaf once the learner holds it. No plan task in the three journeys is ticked, so whether the learner holds any of this is the learner's to say, and this file does not claim it.

**Gap**: no passage in any kind is graded full. A question whose tree has a gap is blocked. None is open now.

**Tier** (every node table): the learner has not stated a phase goal, so every node a question's answer rests on is marked `needed now`. That is a default, not a choice the learner made, and it is set once here for all 46 questions. With a stated goal, some nodes would move to `when it bites` or `link only`, and the short path would be shorter than the full path.

**Minutes** (the `Min` column): estimates, labelled as such. For the nodes of C1 to C3 they were set from the length of the passage plus the worked example done by hand. For every other node the estimate is the words of the passage that teaches it (code blocks and video tables left out) at 120 words a minute, which is slow reading for maths, plus 5 minutes for the worked example by hand, rounded to a whole minute; a node taught by a plan uses that plan's length. N26 and N27 are taught by one passage of about 16 minutes, split 10 and 6 so that a question holding both counts the passage once. The per-question totals are the sums of the node rows, so a node that several questions share is counted in each question's total, not once across the quiz.

**Full**: all five parts of the format's standard are present (what and why in plain words, a worked example with every intermediate value, the method as steps, one failure with its number, a check). Part 3 is accepted when the computation is written as an ordered chain of lines, each with its value, or as a script a reader can rerun on new numbers. The five older vault notes do not number their steps, and read strictly every cell for them would be partial; the notes written for N15, N16, N18, N26 and N27 do number theirs. **Partial** names the missing part. **Mention** means named or used without being taught. **None** means no passage; the cell says why when the kind cannot carry the node.

**Invented outcomes**: the quiz tags 23 questions whose judge outcomes and confidences are made up. Their material is the method (the table, the walk, the resampling), so a node on such a question is graded on whether the method is taught, not on whether any number is real.
