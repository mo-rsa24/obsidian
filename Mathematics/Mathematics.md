# Mathematics

Mathematics that is true without any software and used by more than one field. Each branch below has its own map.

- [[Statistics]]: probability, distributions and inference.
- [[Calculus]]: change and accumulation; integrals over the whole line and the normalising constants they give.
- [[Algebra]]: the rules for manipulating expressions, starting with logarithms.
- [[Trigonometry]]: the cosine and sine as a point on the circle of radius 1, angles in radians, and the quarter turn of the cosine squared that noise schedules are built on.
