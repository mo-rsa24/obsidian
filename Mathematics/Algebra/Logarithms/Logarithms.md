# Logarithms

Up: [[Algebra]] · ⬅️ [[The Exponent Rules]] · [[Arithmetic And Geometric Mean]] ➡️

Mathematics, Algebra, Logarithms. **A logarithm turns multiplication into addition**, which is why a log-density is easier to work with than the density itself.

## Notes in this folder

- [[The Log Of A Power And Of A Quotient]]: the log of a power is the power times the log, and the log of a quotient is a difference, worked on a tempered Gaussian.
- [[Solving For The Exponent With Logarithms]]: solving an equation or inequality with the unknown in the exponent, such as the fewest clean rows for a stated tolerance, with the flip of the sign when dividing by a negative log.
- [[Log-Sum-Exp]]: the log of a sum of exponentials, computed safely by pulling out the largest term, as in a softmax, a mixture density or the ideal expert's log-density.
