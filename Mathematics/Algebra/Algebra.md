# Algebra

Up: [[Mathematics]]

Mathematics, Algebra. The rules for manipulating expressions, one folder per topic.

- [[The Exponent Rules]]: what happens to an exponent when powers of e are multiplied, raised to a power, or given a count of zero or below, worked on the Gaussian N(0, 0.25), whose heights are e to a negative number.
- [[Logarithms]]: what a logarithm does to products, quotients and powers, and how it solves for an unknown exponent.
- [[Arithmetic And Geometric Mean]]: the average of two positive numbers is at least the square root of their product, equal exactly when the numbers are equal; proved from a squared difference, and used to show the factor q in kappa = q times phi is at most 1.
- [[The Binomial Theorem]]: how a power of a sum such as (2 + 3)^4 multiplies out, with each term's coefficient counting the picks that give it, read off Pascal's triangle; worked to 625, broken by dropping the coefficients, and checked by chances that add to 1.
