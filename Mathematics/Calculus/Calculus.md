# Calculus

Mathematics, Calculus. The topics of change and accumulation, one folder each.

- [[Integration]]: areas under curves, the integrals that have no antiderivative to write down, and how their values are found anyway.
- [[Sequences And Series]]: what an endless list closes in on, and functions written as endless sums: the Maclaurin series for $e^x$, $\cosh x$, $\log(1 + y)$ and $\log\cosh x$, and the limit $(1 + \frac{a}{n})^n \to e^a$.
