# Differential Equations

Up: [[Calculus]]

Mathematics, Calculus, Differential Equations.

**A differential equation says how a quantity changes rather than what it is, and solving one means finding the path that obeys that rule from a starting value. Diffusion models run one of each: a stochastic equation that adds noise and an ordinary one that removes it deterministically.**

- [[Ordinary Differential Equation]]: An ordinary differential equation states how a quantity changes with one variable, such as dx/dt = f(x, t), and solving it means finding the path x(t) that obeys it from a starting value.
- [[Stochastic Differential Equation]]: A stochastic differential equation is an ordinary differential equation with a random noise term added, dx = f(x, t) dt + g(t) dW, so its solution is a random path.
