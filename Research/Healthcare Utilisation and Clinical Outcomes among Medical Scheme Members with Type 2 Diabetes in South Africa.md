# Diabetes Mellitus
Diabetes Mellitus: Many people have it, most have type 2
- metabolic disorder  [[Diffusion Models#^e3cf9f|hello]]
	- chronic high blood glucose levels
	- disruptions in carb, fats, protein metabolism
- caused by defects in insulin production
- stats: variation due to differences in population demographics, geographic settings, diagnostic methods

# ***Diabetes Control & Complications***
**complication:** 
- **causes:** poorly controlled diabetes
- **result:** increasing morbidity, poor quality of life and premature death

# **diabetes control**
- **managing diabetes:** Control of HbA1c, blood pressure and cholesterol levels, regular clinician follow up
- **glycaemic control:** manage blood glucose levels
	- **Goal:** 1) lower blood glucose levels - alleviate hyperglycaemia symptoms
		- 2) Prevent micro(marco) vascular complications
	- **Glycated Haemoglobin (HbA1c):** marker for long-term glycaemic control

## **Healthcare resource utilisation**
**HRU:** scope & type of accessible healthcare services 
- **scope:** prevention, diagnosis, treatment and ongoing health maintenance
**Patterns:** How someone uses healthcare services over time 

### **Importance of HRU patterns:** 
- **how they help:** for designing interventions that
	- enhance access to healthcare, improve health outcomes, cost effective care delivery

**Contributors to complications and high death:**
- undiagnosed and poorly controlled diabetes
- **driven by:** barriers to diagnosis and treatment
